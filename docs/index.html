Story Boarder

by Black Flame Studios

A whiteboard for planning stories. Lay out plots, characters and worlds with sticky notes, frames, text, pictures and arrows on an infinite, zoomable board. Works with a finger on phones and tablets, and with a mouse and keyboard on Windows.

  • Sticky notes whose text shrinks to fit, in eight colors
  • Frames that group notes and move with them: chapters, acts, characters, locations
  • Arrows between notes, to map cause and effect or who knows whom
  • Pictures from your device, for maps, mood boards and character art
  • Search, undo and redo, and export any part of a board as a PNG picture
  • Import whiteboards exported as CSV, laid out automatically
  • No account and no cloud: boards are saved as files on your device
