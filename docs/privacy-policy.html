Privacy Policy

Story Boarder, by Black Flame Studios · Effective November 13, 2026

Story Boarder is a whiteboard app for planning stories, for Android and Windows. This policy explains what information the app handles and who it goes to. In short: your boards never leave your device unless you share them yourself. The free Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show them.

Your boards

Everything you put on a board (notes, frames, text, pictures and arrows) is stored only on your device: in files you choose to save, and, on Android, in the app's private storage so your work survives the app being closed. Black Flame Studios has no servers and no accounts. We never receive, see or store your boards. Files you import or open are read on your device only.

Ads (free Android app)

The Android app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches and ad views and taps;
  • Diagnostic information about how the app and the ads perform.

This data goes to Google, not to Black Flame Studios. It is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before loading ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from the app's ☰ menu → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Premium (no ads)

You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Premium and keeps the answer on your device. Once you have Premium, the app no longer requests ads or shows the ad consent form.

Windows app

The Windows app shows no ads, makes no network connections and collects no data. Your boards stay in the files you save.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to check your Premium purchase with Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. When you open, save or import a file, you pick it yourself in Android's file picker, and the app can only use the files you pick.

Children

Story Boarder is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep no personal data, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the app stores on your device, uninstall it or clear its storage in Android settings. Files you saved yourself stay where you saved them. For data collected by Google for ads, see My Ad Center and Google's privacy policy.

Changes
