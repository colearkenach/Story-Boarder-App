# Google Play store listing

Copy and paste into Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**. Limits are Google's;
the counts are checked.

## App name (max 30)

```
Story Boarder
```

A longer name helps people find the app in search, e.g. `Story Boarder: Story Planner` (28). Google allows a short
descriptive phrase but not keyword lists.

## Short description (max 80)

```
Plan your story on an infinite board of sticky notes, frames, arrows & pictures.
```

## Full description (max 4000)

```
Story Boarder is a whiteboard made for planning stories. Lay out your plot, characters and world on an infinite board, move things around until the story clicks, and keep it all in your pocket.

Put anything on the board
• Sticky notes in eight colors, with text that shrinks to fit
• Frames that group notes into acts, chapters, characters or places. Move a frame and everything in it comes along
• Arrows between notes, for cause and effect, timelines, or who knows whom
• Text for titles and headings
• Pictures from your device: maps, mood boards, character art

Made for touch
• Drag to pan, pinch to zoom
• Double-tap the board to add a note, or a note to edit it
• Drag the dots around a note to draw an arrow, or drop it on empty space to add a connected note
• Long-press anything for more options
• Select several items at once to move, recolor, duplicate or delete them together

And also
• Undo and redo
• Search your board and jump from match to match
• Jump straight to any frame
• Export the whole board, or just part of it, as a picture to share
• Import whiteboards exported as CSV, with frames and notes laid out for you
• Your work is kept if Android closes the app in the background

Your boards stay yours
No account, no sign-in, no cloud. Boards are saved as files on your device, where you can back them up, share them, or open them in Story Boarder for Windows.

Story Boarder is free with a small banner ad. A one-time purchase removes the ads for good.
```
